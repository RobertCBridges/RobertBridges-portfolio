Quadplane VTOL Drone

Jan 2024 - Feb 2026 • Designer • Drone, Mechanical

Prototype Vertical Take-Off and Landing capable drone.

Project details

Overview

Design and production of a VTOL drone that switches between quadcopter and fixed-wing modes. Take-off and landing are handled vertically to broaden their location possibilities, once airborne it transitions to plane mode for a more efficient cruise, higher speeds and extended range. First flight was successfully completed in February 2026. All of it's specifications were validated during it's second test flight in july of 2026.

Key features / highlights

  • 3m Wingspan
  • 500g Payload

Differential Drive Mobile Robot

Spring 2026 • Engineer • Robotics, ROS2, Real Time

A differential drive mobile robot designed for autonomous navigation and obstacle avoidance.

Project details

Overview

Differential drive mobile robot designed for autonomous navigation and obstacle avoidance using ROS2. The robot follows a two layer philosophy: the first, a lower layer comprised of a custom designed PCB handles low level control and perception whilst the second made of a raspberry pi manages more computationally heavy higher level functions. The robot is designed to be compact and efficient, whilst using well known hardware and software to focus on maintainability.

Key features / highlights

  • Encoder based odometry
  • Autonomous navigation using the NAV2 stack
  • Interruption and timing Management
  • Compact size

F.O.C.A. — Original Spacecraft Mission Design

Jan – May 2026 • Spacecraft Systems Designer • spacecraft design, orbital mechanics, systems engineering

Original spacecraft design to intercept foreign objects in orbit, carried through SRR, SDR, and PDR review gates.

Project details

Overview

F.O.C.A. is an original spacecraft mission design concept created to intercept foreign objects in orbit. Using prior flight missions as a baseline, I developed the vehicle architecture and defined a full mission life timeline, from launch through end-of-mission. Large portions of the spacecraft were designed using common geometry like honeycomb pannels. The design was validated through a full systems engineering review process, progressing through System Requirements Review (SRR), System Definition Review (SDR), and Preliminary Design Review (PDR) gates.

Key features / highlights

  • Vehicle architecture based on prior flight mission baselines
  • Full mission life timeline, from launch to end-of-mission
  • Authored SRR, SDR, and PDR documentation to validate the design
  • Weighted trade-offs

Tech & tools

  • Fusion 360
  • Systems engineering review process (SRR/SDR/PDR)
  • MATLAB
  • Python

My role

Designed the spacecraft's vehicle architecture and authored the SRR, SDR, and PDR documentation used to validate the design and define the mission life timeline.
