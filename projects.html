F.O.C.A. — Original Spacecraft Mission Design

Jan – May 2026 • Spacecraft Systems Designer • spacecraft design, orbital mechanics, systems engineering

Original spacecraft design to intercept foreign objects in orbit, carried through SRR, SDR, and PDR review gates.

Project details

Overview

F.O.C.A. is an original spacecraft mission design concept created to intercept foreign objects in orbit. Using prior flight missions as a baseline, I developed the vehicle architecture and defined a full mission life timeline, from launch through end-of-mission. Large portions of the spacecraft were designed using common geometry like honeycomb pannels. The design was validated through a full systems engineering review process, progressing through System Requirements Review (SRR), System Definition Review (SDR), and Preliminary Design Review (PDR) gates.

Key features / highlights

  • Vehicle architecture based on prior flight mission baselines
  • Full mission life timeline, from launch to end-of-mission
  • Authored SRR, SDR, and PDR documentation to validate the design
  • Weighted trade-offs

Tech & tools

  • Fusion 360
  • Systems engineering review process (SRR/SDR/PDR)
  • MATLAB
  • Python

My role

Designed the spacecraft's vehicle architecture and authored the SRR, SDR, and PDR documentation used to validate the design and define the mission life timeline.

Focus Glasses

Aug – Dec 2025 • Team Lead, Product Design • product design, market research, finite element analysis

A wearable product concept that uses invisible spectral flicker to help the wearer keep focus, developed with a 4-person team from market research through a full Product Design Specification.

Project details

Overview

Focus Glasses is an innovative wearable concept designed to help the user keep focus by making use of invisible spectral flicker. Working with a 4-person team, I led market research to shape the product direction and helped deliver a full Product Design Specification. To ensure the design was safe to wear, I completed finite element analysis on the glasses. The project was presented at a public exhibit open to students, professors, and the community, where it was voted best new product in the class.

Key features / highlights

  • Uses invisible spectral flicker to support user focus
  • Delivered a complete Product Design Specification with a 4-person team
  • Finite element analysis performed to validate wearer safety
  • Voted best new product in the class at a public exhibit

Tech & tools

  • Ansys (FEA)
  • Fusion 360
  • Market research
  • Product Design Specification (PDS)

My role

Led market research for the team and helped deliver the Product Design Specification. Completed the finite element analysis used to ensure the safety of the glasses.
